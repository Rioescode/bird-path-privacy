Bird Path — User Data Deletion

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This page applies to Bird Path (package/app ID: com.birdpath.game).

Bird Path does not have user accounts and does not store personal data on our servers. Your game progress, unlocked skins, and mute preference are stored only on your device. Uninstalling Bird Path removes this local data.

Request deletion

To request deletion of any data associated with Bird Path, email esriorio@gmail.com with the subject Bird Path data deletion. Include your Google Play account email or another identifying detail so we can understand and process your request.

We will respond to your request within 30 days.

For more information, see our Privacy Policy.
